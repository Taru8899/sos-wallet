About the Signature

Every offer requires a signature that mints 1 SOS to you — the only way to post. It proves authorship (not a contract). Full text is stored as signedMessage and can be verified against your wallet via “Verify sig”.

⚠ Safety Rules

Pure P2P, no escrow. SOS mints and tx hashes are evidence only, not protection.
Posting or accepting mints 1 SOS to that party.
Deliver SOS first on-chain; only then send payment. Poster marks Closed after.
Sell offer accepted → you mint SOS to buyer first; expect payment only after confirmation.
Negative Effective balance (Trust − Push) blocks selling. Buy/accept to raise it.
Flag no-pay reports non-payers (needs your mint tx as evidence) but does not reverse the deal.
Trust = total SOS received.
Push = SOS you minted via actions.
Effective = Trust − Push (genuine unspent activity).
