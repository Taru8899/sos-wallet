⚠ Safety Rules

  • This is pure P2P. There is no escrow.
  • Posting an offer mints 1 SOS to your wallet (on-chain proof you paid gas to list).
  • Always verify the poster’s SOS balance on Etherscan before sending anything.
  • Never send first to a stranger. Prefer public channels (Telegram / X) with reputation.
  • Goods & services deals are highest risk — use common sense.

About optional signature

When you choose Sign & Post, your wallet signs a short text that contains the offer details (type, amount, price, method, contact, your address, timestamp).

Anyone can later click Verify sig. The page recovers the address that produced the signature. If it matches the poster address, the listing was created by that wallet and was not altered afterwards.

A signature does not lock tokens, create escrow, or force anyone to trade. It only proves authorship of the text.
