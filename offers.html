About the Signature

Every offer is signed and mints 1 SOS to you at the same time — this is the only way to post. Signing proves you personally authored the offer; it is not a binding contract. The full signed text is stored alongside the offer as signedMessage, and anyone can verify it against your wallet address using the "Verify sig" button.

⚠ Safety Rules — Please Read Before the Use

This is a pure peer-to-peer marketplace — there is no escrow. Minted SOS and transaction hashes serve only as evidence of activity, not as protection against fraud.

Posting an offer always mints 1 SOS to you, and requires a signature that proves you authored it — this is the only way to post. Negotiate details off-app using the contact info provided. Accepting an offer mints 1 SOS to the accepting party.

The party delivering SOS should act first on-chain. Only after that should payment be sent. The poster should then mark the deal Closed.

If you post a sell offer and someone accepts it, you (the seller) mint SOS to them first — expect payment only after that mint is confirmed.

Negative Effective balance blocks selling — and signals you need to buy. If Trust − Push goes negative, you've pushed out more than you've genuinely received, so the app won't let you post a sell offer. This is also a signal, not just a restriction: buy mints (accept a buy offer, or get accepted on a sell offer) to add to your Trust and raise your Effective balance back above zero. Once it's positive, selling is available again.

Flag no-pay lets you report a non-paying counterparty, but it does not close or reverse the deal. You must supply your own mint transaction as evidence before flagging.
